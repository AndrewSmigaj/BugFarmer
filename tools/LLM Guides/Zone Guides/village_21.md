BugFarmer — Village & Fly Meadow Zone Generation Guide

(Authoritative, LLM-Readable, Polished)

This guide defines how to generate the Starting Village Zone, which exists within a Fly Meadow biome. The village is a fixed, shared-world structure populated by NPCs and infrastructure. Players do not own buildings here and do not receive housing in this zone.

The purpose of this zone is to:

Establish the game’s tone and physical language

Introduce fly ecology through natural causes

Anchor navigation, trade, and learning

Feel inhabited, imperfect, and alive

No scripted events. No safe zones. No artificial ecology props.

1. Zone Identity

Biome: Fly Meadow

Difficulty: Easy

World Role: Starting hub, early economy, ecological introduction

The meadow is primary.
The village is a human interruption, not a replacement.

Grass, flowers, flies, predators, rot, and decay all exist inside and around town.

2. High-Level Spatial Regions

The zone blends five regions without hard borders:

Village Core (Civic & Trade)

Residential Service Buildings

Orchard

Fly Farm (Outside Village)

Meadow Transition

Density, materials, and clutter define boundaries — not walls.

3. Roads & Movement
Road Rules

Grid-aligned only (no rotation variance)

Roads guide movement, not safety

Roads degrade as they exit town

Materials

Stone path: Village core

Dirt path: Outer village

Trampled grass: Meadow transition

Required Connections

North–south road through town

East–west connector

Spurs to orchard and fly farm

Roads should:

Connect buildings logically

Avoid perfect symmetry

Occasionally end awkwardly or fade out

9. Orchard (Structured Agriculture)

The orchard is intentionally artificial and ordered.

Layout

Grid-aligned rows of apple trees

Clear walking lanes

Apple Lifecycle (Critical)

Apples fall naturally

Apples start fresh

Over time they age

Eventually they rot

Rotting apples attract flies

Props

Crates at row ends

Ladders

Compost piles

Fallen apples under trees

No placed “rot plants”.
Rot emerges from fruit age.

10. Fly Farm (Outside Village)

This is a landscape system, not a building.

Core Elements

Compost piles

Concentrated fallen apples

Darkened soil

Occasional shallow puddles

Infrastructure

Net posts

Hanging bait baskets

Collection trays

Simple wooden frames

Failure Evidence

Broken nets

Overrun areas

Predator signs

This area teaches fly farming by existing.

11. Meadow Integration

Inside the village:

Flowers still grow

Flies still spawn

Grass is uneven

Near buildings:

Flowers thin

Dirt increases

Trampling visible

Outside village:

Flower density increases

Apples and rot more common

Structures fade away

12. Plants as Ecology Hooks

Plants drive insect behavior via density and proximity.

Required plants:

Meadow flowers (multiple types)

Tall grass

Orchard trees

Plants are never scripted triggers.

13. Lighting (Non-Protective)

Lamps near doors and roads

Sparse, uneven

Can be removed

No safety guarantees

Some bugs dislike light.
If players remove lights, consequences are allowed.

14. Decoration Philosophy

Decorations must imply:

Use

Wear

Intent

Failure

Prefer:

Broken variants

Used variants

Slight disorder

Avoid:

Perfect symmetry

Pristine fantasy props

Decorative clutter with no story

15. Sprite Generation Guidance (Embedded)

When generating sprites:

Favor variants, not new mechanics

Add wear states

Show contents where possible

Respect grid alignment

Never include roofs

16. Hard Constraints (Locked)

❌ No player housing
❌ No roofs
❌ No safe zones
❌ No scripted events
❌ No artificial rot props

Everything emerges from layout + time + ecology.

17. Validation Checklist (LLM)

Before exporting:

 Village clearly readable

 Orchard structured

 Apples fall and rot naturally

 Fly farm exists outside town

 Roads connect logically

 Decorations imply use

 Meadow ecology remains active inside village

If something feels empty, add physical evidence, not systems.
