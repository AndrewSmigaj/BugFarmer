# 2026-08-05 — three ant-carapace designs, NO design direction

> *"can you please just do three ant carapace armor versions without telling it what to put other than
> the sprite and it is made from ant parts and carapace"*

**One paid call.** The prompt says only: armour made from ant parts — chitin, shell, carapace, mandibles,
leg segments — worn by a person, not an ant costume. The three design slots just say *"your own design"*,
*"clearly different"*, *"clearly different again"*. **No reference figure** either; a reference would steer
it, which is the opposite of the point.

The technical constraints stayed — magenta background, armless, silhouette-over-detail at 40px, no magenta
on the figure. Those are quality rules, not design direction.

| | what it produced |
|---|---|
| **1** | dark red — antennae curving up from the helm, barred visor, segmented plates, tasset skirt |
| **2** | tan/ochre — a hooded carapace helm with mandible fangs framing a dark eye slit, segmented abdomen |
| **3** | charcoal with **gold** mandibles curving up like horns, smooth dark plates |

## Worth noticing

**Undirected came out better than directed.** These are more characterful and more internally consistent
than either batch where the three designs were specified. Writing three design briefs narrowed what it
could reach for — the descriptions became a ceiling rather than a floor.

That is a real argument for opening with an undirected round and only writing briefs when a specific thing
is wanted, rather than always prescribing three parents up front.
