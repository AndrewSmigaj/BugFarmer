## ⚠ Defect in option 3

**665 magenta pixels are painted onto the figure** — on the boots and one on the helm. The model does this
when it uses the key colour as a highlight; the design guide warns about it and says to check every sheet.
They survive keying (the flood-fill only removes magenta reachable from the border), so they would render
as bright magenta specks on the armour.

Fixable — either a repaint pass, or regenerate that one option with an explicit "no magenta anywhere on
the figure" line. Not a reason to reject the design, but it has to be dealt with if 3 is the pick.
