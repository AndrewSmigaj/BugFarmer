# ant-carapace — candidate batches

Two colourways to lock in: **red fire-ant** and **black ant**. Every batch is undirected — the prompt gives
only the material and the colour, because that beat every directed round. `A` and `B` are two separate
rolls of the same prompt, for variety.

| batch | colour | face | h-spread | w-spread | baselines | stray magenta |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `2026-08-05-red-fireant-faces-A` | red | ✅ | 1.3% | 11.3% | aligned | **0** |
| `2026-08-05-red-fireant-faces-B` | red | ✅ | 0.8% | 3.9% | 9px apart | ⚠ **374** |
| `2026-08-05-black-ant-faces-A` | black | ✅ | 4.0% | 4.4% | 2px apart | ⚠ **105** |
| `2026-08-05-black-ant-faces-B` | black | ✅ | 2.4% | 11.0% | aligned | **0** |

**Stray magenta** = pixels of the key colour painted onto the figure itself. They survive keying, because
the flood-fill only removes magenta reachable from the border, so they would render as bright specks on the
armour. Cleanable, but it is real work — worth preferring a clean batch where the designs are equally good.
