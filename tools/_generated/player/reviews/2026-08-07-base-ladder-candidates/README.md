## My read — you decide

- **leather** — all three landed and are genuinely different. The best sheet of the five.
- **steel** — also strong. ⚠ Option 3's open helm came back as a **black void** where the face should be.
  Reads as a shadowed opening at game size, but it is a defect, not a choice.
- **wood** — option 1 (bark plates) is the only one that reads as *armour*. Options 2 and 3 came out
  comic — 2 is literally a basket, and 3 grew a log helm despite the brief saying no helm at all. If you
  want wood to be a serious rung, this may want a re-roll with 1 as the anchor.
- **copper / iron** — look at them yourself before I colour your read.
