# Ant Colony — Flora brainstorm

The ant colony has no surface plants — it is an excavated nest in dirt and clay. Its "flora" is what the
ants **farm and herd**: a leafcutter-style **fungus garden** (the colony's pantry), the **aphid livestock**
clustered on **root tendrils** they suck for sap, and the molds/slimes/glow that creep through the damp,
warm dark. Variety here is the point — a leafcutter fungus garden in nature is a layered, terraced, many-
chambered organ. Notes flag honeydew / edible / alchemy hooks for the farming + apothecary loops.

---

## The cultivated FUNGUS garden (leafcutter-style, many varieties + terraces)

The ants chew leaf fragments into a substrate and grow fungus on it in stacked spongy terraces. This is
the heart of the zone's flora — go wide.

### Staple crop fungi (the colony's food)
- `pale_brood_fungus` — soft chalk-white spongy fungus in dense cushions; the colony's staple. *Edible
  (bland, filling), the base "crop" of the garden.*
- `gromphadina_cap` — fat low cream-colored caps clustered on terraces; the prized eating fungus. *Edible,
  higher value.*
- `gongylidia_nodes` — clusters of tiny swollen nutrient-bulbs the ants pinch off and eat (the real
  leafcutter staple). *Edible, the literal "fruit" of the garden; nurse-ant feed.*
- `staghorn_fungus` — pale branching antler-like fungus rising from a terrace. *Edible, decorative once tamed.*
- `terrace_mycelium` — the white fuzzy mat covering a fresh leaf-substrate bed; pre-harvest growth stage.
- `gray_button_fungus` — humble gray nub-mushrooms in gravelly clay; low-tier filler crop.

### Glowing / luminous fungi (the nest's only light)
- `glowcap_lantern` — a domed mushroom glowing soft warm-yellow; the ants tend it near nurseries for
  warmth-light. *Light source; alchemy: glow-extract.*
- `foxfire_shelf` — pale green bioluminescent bracket fungus climbing root-pillars; ghostly cold glow.
  *Alchemy (glow ink, lures); edible-risky.*
- `bluecap_glowmoss` — cushiony fungus-moss with electric-blue points of light along damp seams.
- `ember_fungus` — orange-red glowing caps that pulse faintly like coals; warmest light in the brood
  chambers. *Alchemy: heat-essence.*
- `spore_lantern_pod` — a swollen translucent pod that glows brighter when disturbed and puffs spores.
  *Alchemy; hazard (spore cloud).*

### Mold, slime molds, rot
- `creeping_white_mold` — invasive fuzzy white mold that smothers terraces if the garden is untended; the
  "weed" of the fungus farm (the out-of-balance state when workers die). *Pest/weed.*
- `escovopsis_blight` — sickly yellow-green parasitic mold that infects and rots the crop fungus; the
  leafcutter's real nemesis. *Disease vector; alchemy: toxin.*
- `dog_vomit_slime` — bright lurid-yellow slime mold oozing across a wet wall, slowly flowing. *Alchemy
  (reagent); harmless oddity.*
- `lacework_slime` — a delicate fan of bright slime-mold veins spreading over leaf litter; pulses, then
  fruits into tiny stalks.
- `red_rust_mold` — rusty crumbling mold on old dead fungus terraces; marks abandoned garden chambers.
- `black_spore_crust` — a hard black crust of dead spores sealing a collapsed chamber; ominous.
- `weeping_fungus` — fungus that drips slow droplets of dark fluid; floor beneath stays slick. *Alchemy.*

### Substrate & cultivation matter
- `leaf_mash_substrate` — the wet green-brown pulp of chewed leaf fragments the fungus grows on; fresh beds.
- `chewed_leaf_pile` — a mound of half-shredded leaf scraps brought in by leafcutters, awaiting mashing.
- `compost_terrace` — a lower terrace of darker, spent substrate being recycled; warm, rich, fly-attracting.
- `forage_petal_heap` — bright flower petals dragged down from the surface, vivid against the dark. *Edible
  trim; pretty decor once tamed.*

## The herded APHID "livestock" + their root-tendril pasture

The ants ranch aphids on living roots, milking honeydew. The aphids themselves are bugs (see the bugs doc);
here is the **plant** they cluster and feed on, plus the honeydew product as a "harvest."

- `root_tendril` — a pale fibrous living root threading down a clay wall, the aphids' grazing surface; the
  pasture's "grass." *The thing aphids drain — disrupt it and the herd starves.*
- `sap_root_swollen` — a thick juice-engorged root the aphids favor most; glistens with welling sap. *Best
  pasture; edible sap, alchemy.*
- `weeping_root_node` — a knot in a root where sap beads and drips; honeydew-rich grazing spot.
- `glassy_taproot` — a translucent amber-colored taproot you can see fluid moving through; rare, prized.
  *Alchemy (clarified sap).*
- `honeydew_dew` — beaded sticky-sweet droplets the aphids excrete, clinging to roots and leaves; the
  product the ants farm. *Edible sweet good; cooking sweetener; the colony's "honey."*
- `honeydew_pool` — a small glistening puddle of pooled honeydew on a pasture ledge; rich harvest. *Edible/
  trade good.*
- `sooty_mold_film` — black sooty mold that grows on spilled honeydew (a real aphid-pasture byproduct);
  marks an over-grazed wall. *Weed/pest indicator.*
- `gall_knot` — a swollen woody gall on a root where insects nested; can be pried open for grubs. *Bait/food.*

## Fungal & root "wild" growth (uncultivated, deeper/wilder)

Beyond the tended garden, the nest's damp dark grows its own things.

- `cave_liverwort` — flat dark-green liverwort hugging seeping clay; thrives on nest humidity.
- `pale_nest_moss` — colorless damp moss in soft cushions on chamber floors; soft underfoot, dampens sound.
- `mineral_fuzz` — fine white crystalline fungus-like fuzz on the deepest ore-studded clay (where the nest
  meets the mining tier). *Alchemy.*
- `dripstone_algae` — slick pale algal film on wet dripping clay near the deepest sump.
- `puffball_clutch` — a cluster of clay-colored puffballs that burst into spore-dust when stepped on.
  *Hazard; alchemy (spore powder).*
- `nest_lichen` — crusty gray-green lichen on old pebble-arch stones; very slow, marks ancient tunnels.
- `bone_fungus` — eerie white fungus fruiting from the husk of a dead beetle or a lost miner's remains.
  *Grim; alchemy (rare).*

## Notes
- **Honeydew** is the cross-cutting sweet good (cooking + trade); ranching aphids is the emergent
  "bug-farming" hook the zone teases — tame a herd and you farm honeydew yourself.
- **Glow fungi** double as the zone's lighting and as alchemy reagents (glow ink, lures, heat-essence).
- **Molds/slimes** are the *out-of-balance* visuals: kill the worker ants and `creeping_white_mold` +
  `escovopsis_blight` overrun the terraces (ties to `ecology_proposal.md`).
- **Edible-once-tamed:** several fungi (`gromphadina_cap`, `gongylidia_nodes`, `staghorn_fungus`) and the
  honeydew goods could become exotic farm crops if the player learns colony husbandry.
