# Ant Colony — Trees brainstorm

There are no true trees underground. The colony's "trees" are **what reaches into the dark from above** —
the great living root pillars of surface trees, descending floor-to-ceiling through the nest — and the
**fungal "trees"** the ants cultivate in the garden. Brief and creative: these are the vertical organic
landmarks that give a claustrophobic nest its scale.

---

## Root pillars (the real "trees" of the nest)
- `great_root_pillar` — an enormous tree root, thick as a trunk, descending floor-to-ceiling through a
  chamber; the ants tunnel around and through it. The nest's defining vertical landmark.
- `root_pillar_braided` — several roots twisted into a single braided column, the nest carved around them.
- `taproot_descent` — a single massive taproot plunging straight down into the deepest dark, vanishing
  below; suggests there's *more* nest beneath.
- `root_arch` — a root that loops down and back up, forming a natural archway a worker column streams through.
- `root_curtain` — a hanging veil of fine hair-roots draping across a passage; you push through them.
- `weeping_taproot` — a sap-bleeding root pillar that drips honeydew-ready sap; aphids cluster all over it.

## Petrified / dead roots
- `petrified_root` — a root long dead and mineralized into stone, gray and cold; cracks studded with crystal.
- `hollow_root_husk` — a dead root rotted hollow into a vertical tube the ants use as a chimney/shaft.
- `charred_root` — a blackened root scorched by some old underground fire; brittle, soot-dusted.
- `crystal_veined_root` — a petrified root shot through with glittering mineral veins (deep-tier, near ore).

## Fungal "trees" of the garden
- `fungus_tree_staghorn` — a tall branching antler-fungus grown waist-high, the garden's canopy specimen.
- `bracket_tower` — stacked shelf fungi climbing a root pillar like a spiral staircase of plates.
- `glowcap_tree` — a tall stalked glowing mushroom, head-height, lighting a garden terrace like a lamp-tree.
- `puffstalk` — a tall thin fungal stalk topped with a spore-head that bursts if knocked; a fragile "sapling."
- `mycelium_pillar` — a column entirely overgrown in white mycelium, soft and furred, a ghost of a root.

---

## Notes
- Root pillars set the **vertical scale** and sell the "you've dug beneath a forest" feel; they also double
  as aphid pasture surfaces (`weeping_taproot`) and the structural anchors chambers are carved around.
- Petrified/crystal roots bridge the nest toward the **mining tier** above/below (ore-studded deep clay).
- Fungal "trees" are tall garden occupants — landmark-scale flora that read as canopy in the terraces.
