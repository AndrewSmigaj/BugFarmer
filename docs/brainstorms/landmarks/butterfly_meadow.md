# Brainstorm: Butterfly Meadow — Landmarks & Little Features

Named points of interest for the **Butterfly Meadow** (`butterfly_meadow_11`) — the "little features"
that make the meadow feel like a *place* you can navigate by memory rather than a uniform field of
scatter. The zone design (`butterfly_meadow_11.md`) seeds the iconic ones; this brainstorm is generous
and evocative, adding many more.

A landmark = a small authored composition (a cluster of existing/proposed occupants + flora + bugs)
with a name, a hook, and an ecology or story tie. Organized south→north so the difficulty/feel gradient
reads as you walk it. Most reuse existing ids (flora/bugs/decor/structures catalogs); proposed new ids
are noted inline.

---

## Iconic / headline (from the zone design)
- **The Great Milkweed Stand** — an oversized cluster of `milkweed` (+ `milkweed_common`,
  `milkweed_swamp`) swarming with `caterpillar_monarch`, `chrysalis_monarch`, and a cloud of
  `butterfly_monarch`. *The zone's signature spot; the keystone made visible. Lose it and the monarchs
  go. Likely a quest/photo target for the Ecologist.*
- **Flower-Clock Glade** — a near-perfect ring of wildflowers arranged by color (a "sundial of blooms"):
  `flower_red`, `flower_yellow`, `flower_blue`, `flower_aster`, `oxeye_daisy`, `poppy`, `cornflower`
  around a central `oxeye_daisy` or a small `sundial` decor. *A wonder-spot; flowers open in sequence
  with the day. A natural pollinator magnet (top nectar density).*
- **The Broken Fence Line** — a half-collapsed `fence_wood` / split-rail run swallowed by `tall_grass`,
  with `plantain_weed`, `poppy`, and a leaning `gate_wood` that opens onto nothing. *The failed-farm
  story prop — someone tried to tame this meadow and lost. `spider_orb` strings webs between the rails;
  `butterfly_red_admiral` basks on the sun-warmed wood.*
- **The Basking Boulder** — a big mossy `boulder` (proposed) in a sunbeam, ringed with `moss_clump` and
  `creeping_thyme`. *Butterflies and `beetle_jewel` bask on the warm stone in the morning; a natural
  rest/landmark and a lovely screenshot spot.*
- **The Fallen-Log Bridge** — a long `log_fallen` lying across the boggy seam where meadow meets forest,
  furred with `mushroom_oyster`/`mushroom_turkeytail` and `moss_clump`. *The literal + symbolic crossing
  into harder ground; a `centipede` highway — cross with care.*
- **Forest-Edge Gloom** — the darker north strip itself: dim `grass`, `tree_beech`/`tree_spruce`
  closing the canopy, `fern`/`bracken` underfoot, `stump`s and `log_mossy` hiding `centipede`/`millipede`.
  *Not a single object but a readable region — the "temperature drop" where the meadow ends.*

## South & approach (arrive from the village)
- **The Meadow Gate / Welcome Arch** — a `garden_arch` smothered in `morning_glory` over the southern
  road where you enter from `village_21`. *A threshold marker: "you are leaving town." First flowers,
  first butterflies greet you here.*
- **The Wayside Signpost** — a weathered `signpost` at the road fork pointing "Village ↓ / Forest ↑".
  *Orientation + tone-setting; the forest arrow could be ominously scratched/older.*
- **The First Bloom** — a single radiant `bee_balm` or `butterfly_bush` right at the entrance, always
  busy with pollinators. *A deliberate "wow, look at this" first impression.*

## The meadow proper (the bright middle)
- **The Lavender Drift** — a long sweep of `lavender` + `creeping_thyme` humming with `bee_bumble`/
  `bee_honey`. *A fragrant, purple, bee-dense band; a calming spot and a prime bee-watching place.*
- **The Poppy Field** — a loose splash of `poppy` and `cornflower` over a patch of disturbed ground.
  *Vivid red-and-blue; pioneer flowers reclaiming an old plow scar (story tie to the failed farm).*
- **The Daisy Sea** — a broad drift of `oxeye_daisy` and `chamomile`. *The "lie down in the flowers"
  spot; white-and-gold; gentle.*
- **The Spring & Puddle** — a small clear `water_shallow` seep with `cattail`, `water_lily`/`lily_pad`,
  a leaning `tree_willow_meadow`, and `damselfly`/`dragonfly_meadow` skimming it. *The zone's only
  water; a cool oasis; ties the aquatic-ish bugs in. (Zone design: "maybe a small puddle/spring.")*
- **The Old Apple Tree** — a lone gnarled `tree_apple` dropping `fallen_fruit`, drawing `fly_house`
  and (rarely) the `butterfly_emperor` down from the canopy. *Relic of the failed orchard; a
  fruit-vs-flower ecology micro-story; forage spot.*
- **The Stone Trough** — a cracked old stone water trough (proposed `trough_stone`) half full of
  rainwater, mossed over. *Another failed-farm relic; butterflies "puddle" at its damp rim for minerals
  — a real butterfly behavior, charming to see.*
- **The Honey Hollow** — a wild `beehive`-in-a-`tree_oak` (proposed wild-hive look) dripping comb,
  ringed with `bee_honey`. *The bees' breeding site (`§9.2` needs a hive); a risk-reward forage; the
  yellowjacket raids it — predation made visible.*
- **The Anthill Mound** — a low bare earth mound with a trail of ants crossing the meadow. *Ambient
  life + a callback to the ant-colony zone; ants tend the `aphid_colony` for honeydew (a neat sub-web).*

## Story / human traces (the failed farm + the bug-watcher)
- **The Lepidopterist's Blind** — a small bug-watcher's camp: a `tent` or folding canvas blind,
  `net_post`s, a `stool_wood`, specimen jars on a crate, a `lantern` for moths, a pinned-specimen
  board. *The zone design's named point of interest + a future quest hook (the Ecologist's field camp).
  Moths gather at its lantern at dusk.*
- **The Abandoned Net** — a `broken_net` (**[exists]**) lying in the grass where the blind's owner
  dropped it. *A tiny mystery prop; "what happened to the collector?" flavor.*
- **The Scarecrow Sentinel** — a lopsided `scarecrow` (**[exists]**) left from the failed farm, now
  draped in `ivy`, a butterfly perched on its hat. *Forlorn, gentle; the farm reclaimed by the meadow.*
- **The Furrow Ghosts** — faint parallel ridges of old plow furrows now grassed over, readable only by
  the lines of `poppy`/`plantain_weed` that grow along them. *Subtle environmental storytelling — the
  land remembers being a field.*
- **The Cairn / Boundary Stone** — a small stacked-stone marker (proposed `cairn_stone`) at the old
  field's corner. *A quiet human trace; possibly the lepidopterist's "I was here" marker.*

## The forest edge & gloom (north — the danger ramp)
- **The Wasp Comb** — a grey papery `wasp_paper` nest hanging from a low `tree_hawthorn` branch.
  *A visible "predator lives here" warning; approach and the paper wasps defend it. The easy-wasp
  landmark.*
- **The Yellowjacket Ground-Nest** — a hole in a grassy bank at the edge with `wasp_yellowjacket`
  streaming in and out. *The hard-wasp lair; the most dangerous spot in the bright meadow; a hidden
  threat the player learns to give a wide berth.*
- **The Mushroom Ring** — a `mushroom_fairy_ring` of toadstools in a shaded clearing, with a lone
  `mushroom_fly_agaric` at its center. *Storybook magic + a "don't eat that one" beat; a forage spot.*
- **The Rotting Log Run** — a `log_mossy` + `branch_pile` thick with `mushroom_oyster`, `woodlouse`,
  and a hidden `millipede`. *The decomposition layer; flip it for fungus + risk a millipede. The
  detritivore showcase.*
- **The Hollow Tree** — `tree_hollow` (proposed) with a dark cavity at its base from which a
  `centipede` (or the rare `centipede_tiger`) emerges. *A natural "den" landmark; the scariest edge
  feature; a mini-encounter spot.*
- **The Standing Snag** — a bare `tree_dead` antlered against the sky, bracket fungus on its trunk,
  `bee_carpenter` boring its wood. *The ominous silhouette that says "forest begins"; a navigation
  beacon visible from across the meadow.*
- **The Fern Hollow** — a damp dip filled with `fern_ostrich`/`bracken` and `wood_sorrel`, cool and
  green and quiet. *The gentle face of the forest edge — beautiful, not (yet) dangerous; a
  catch-your-breath spot before the gloom proper.*
- **The Threshold Stumps** — a cluster of `stump`/`stump_mossy` where the meadow's old clearing ends.
  *Marks the true meadow/forest boundary; sit-spots; fungus + lurkers underneath.*

## Optional / event / rare features
- **The Migration Roost** *(seasonal event)* — a `tree_oak`/`tree_pine` cloaked in dozens of clustered
  `butterfly_monarch` resting in passage. *A spectacular seasonal spectacle; a timed photo/collect
  opportunity; the meadow at its most magical.*
- **The Emperor's Oak** — a tall forest-edge `tree_oak` the rare `butterfly_emperor` patrols, baited
  down only by `fallen_fruit` left at its base. *A rare-catch destination; teaches the
  fruit-not-flowers trick.*
- **The Dewy Web Gallery** — a stretch of the broken fence where morning `spider_orb` webs hang heavy
  with dew. *A purely beautiful dawn moment; also where you watch a second predator at work.*
- **The Puddling Club** — a damp mud patch (`mud` tile) where a knot of male butterflies gather to sip
  minerals. *A real behavior staged as a charming little gathering; a reliable multi-catch spot.*

## Notes on use
- These compose from existing/proposed occupants, flora, bugs, decor, and structures — they are
  **authored vignettes**, not new mechanics. The scenes (`scene_butterfly_meadow.py`,
  `scene_meadow_forest_edge.py`) should each stage 2–4 of these so the showcase reads as a *place*.
- The south→north ordering is deliberate: gentle/pretty landmarks dominate the south; threat/decay
  landmarks cluster at the north edge — so simply walking the zone teaches the difficulty gradient.
- Several double as **quest/Ecologist hooks** (Great Milkweed Stand, Lepidopterist's Blind, Wasp Comb,
  Migration Roost) — flag them when the quest system lands.
