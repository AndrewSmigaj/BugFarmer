# Why the village's bugs come first

*Your decision (2026-10-04, recorded in D83): the village's bug life is the first finished slice, done when its
population charts are right. The ants come second.*

### What "a finished slice" means
One small part of the game brought to the final standard, end to end, before going wide: the design written, built,
measured, and played by you. What the first slice teaches becomes the kit for every slice after it: the behaviour
parts, how dangers are signalled, the tuning settings, the arena set-ups and the checks.

### Why the village
- It is the first thing players meet.
- It already has a tuned food web to compare against: flies, butterflies, paper wasps, centipedes, millipedes and
  carrion beetles.
- It has everything the later zones need in small: livestock (the fly farm), danger (wasps, the night centipede),
  and nests.
- It already has tuning runs on record, so every change can be compared with what the game does today.

### What the slice holds
- Wasp nest defence and combat, redone on the behaviour model of the previous page.
- The centipede as the first real fight.
- The fly farm, with catching and subduing (nets, sprays, the bug stick).
- Fair hits for every player, not only the one whose computer runs the zone.
- The known breaks fixed: predators that can breed the moment they appear, a starvation timer that ignores its
  setting, split-off groups that never carry food home, carcasses that vanish after 60 seconds.
- Shallow water stops walking bugs, deep water stops all bugs, and fliers turn away from deep water instead of piling
  up at the shore (your answer of 2026-10-04).

### When it is done
- Without players, each species' numbers rise and fall in a living cycle over about 48 game-days: not a flat line
  pinned at a cap, not a permanent crash. Occasional surges and die-offs are fine, and a species that dies out can
  come back.
- With test players over-hunting and over-farming, collapses are allowed, but they show warning signs the Ecologist
  can read, and there is a way back the Ecologist's quests can reward.
- Two players stay in sync during fights and nest defence, including one who joins late.
- You have played it and signed it off.

The village's layout redesign stays with the zone-design process, separately.
