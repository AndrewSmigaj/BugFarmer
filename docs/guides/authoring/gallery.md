# Gallery — before/after pairs (what "better" means here)

Annotated pairs from real improvement passes on `bee_meadow_20` (2026-07-05/06). Each pair is
ONE principle made visible, with its corrections-ledger pointer. Images in [references/](references/)
(compressed working renders — the live previews under `tools/_generated/previews/zones/` are
always the current truth). Calibrate on these before a pass; add a pair whenever a pass teaches
something a screenshot says better than a sentence.

## 1. The zone: bland grass → regions with jobs
**Before:** [zone_before.jpg](references/zone_before.jpg) — the first composed zone: correct
contracts, lint 0, and ~90% one grass tile. Technically right, nothing worth walking to.
**After:** [zone_mid.jpg](references/zone_mid.jpg) — the same zone after ground-first passes:
forest floors, coves with wrack lines, the inlet, dirt country south, the gorge.
**Principle (C9):** blandness is fixed with GROUND first — materials, masses, transitions.
Object scatter only fixes close-ups; ground reads at every zoom.

## 2. The beach: even spray → the wrack line
**Before:** [beach_before.jpg](references/beach_before.jpg) — shells and driftwood sprinkled
uniformly across the sand: reads as confetti.
**After:** [beach_after.jpg](references/beach_after.jpg) — flotsam concentrated in the 2-3
cells nearest the water (the way tides deposit), dune grass at the seam, the picnic and
shipwreck as NAMED places, buoys off the cove mouths.
**Principle (C5):** structure debris the way the world deposits it; landmarks are placed
deterministically, never dice.

## 3. The hamlet: two houses on grass → one working harbor
**Before:** [hamlet_before.jpg](references/hamlet_before.jpg) — two cottages dropped near a
LANDLOCKED pond, dock detached from the shore, clutter floating in grass.
**After:** [hamlet_after.jpg](references/hamlet_after.jpg) — Gullwash Landing: a true arm of
the sea (open water end to end), one home per shore with DOORS FACING THE WATER (the south
home is the north-door plan), a dock per shore stopping mid-water, the footbridge, the catch
and gear split across two quays.
**Principles (C1, C4, C8):** doors face context; harbors connect to the sea (verify IN TEXT);
a pier that reaches the far shore is a bridge.

## 4. The apiary: boxes on a lawn → a workplace
**Before:** [apiary_before.jpg](references/apiary_before.jpg) — five hive boxes floating in an
oversized empty pen.
**After:** [apiary_after.jpg](references/apiary_after.jpg) — a dirt working-strip under the
hive row, worn lanes gate→work corner, the extraction line (extractor→table→shelf→rack),
PACKED planted flower-bed rows against the wild meadow ring, the bee waterer, and the wild
hive hanging in an oak just OUTSIDE the fence.
**Principles (C6 + the Local lens):** rows for human things, clumps for wild — and the border
between them is the story; every workplace shows its work.

## 5. The gorge: hand-set ore line → doctrine veins
**Before:** [gorge_before.jpg](references/gorge_before.jpg) — masses floating off the banks,
a hand-placed silver/gold/ruby line, stepped bank stones ("spread out in a linear fashion" —
the owner correction that seeded C3).
**After:** [gorge_after.jpg](references/gorge_after.jpg) — masses pressed against both banks,
commons + short core-band rares via `rock_mass(vein_spec=…)`, ore visibly breaching the mass
ring (the research invitation rule), Halloway's claim clustered as ONE campsite.
**Principles (C2, C3):** ore is only veins, measured with the probe; a micro-story's props
huddle — scattered props read as litter.

## 6. The south band: the invented set-piece → the owner's gradient
**Before (a CAUTION, not a win):** [antcountry_after.jpg](references/antcountry_after.jpg) —
"THE OLD DIG": seven same-size discs in a row, mounds, a dig-site story. Built with craft,
REJECTED by the owner — the whole ant framing was builder invention from one sentence
("there is no ant colony in the zone"), and the discs-on-lawn read drew "just sudden
changes no gradients, unatural geometry."
**After:** [southband_after.jpg](references/southband_after.jpg) — the owner's actual design:
a noise-warped material GRADIENT (grass → speckle → dirt tongues → rocky grit; the frontier
meanders, never a level line), five size-varied masses EMBEDDED in the field (one overlapping
cluster, ragged stone hearts, weathering gaps), torn-ground rubble, dry flora, patchy woods
with real clearings holding the east.
**Principles (C12, C13):** a neighbor transition is a gradient of MATERIALS, never the
neighbor's content built early; blobs stamped N times read as polka dots — vary size,
cluster, embed, and let a field carry them.

## 7. The whole zone after the cold grade
**After:** [zone_after.jpg](references/zone_after.jpg) — the zone once a COLD grading agent
(with village_21_B as the reference bar) drove the last pass: waypointed roads with worn
edges instead of a 145-cell runway, flower/clover/tall-grass DRIFTS on nearly every open
screen, the gorge told in materials (bank lips, gap-toothed rim boulders, scree), coherent
forest floors, stream banks alive with reeds.
**Principle:** never self-grade a lens pass — the taste being calibrated can't certify
itself. A cold agent with a reference zone found four BROKENs the builder's own pass called
fine.
