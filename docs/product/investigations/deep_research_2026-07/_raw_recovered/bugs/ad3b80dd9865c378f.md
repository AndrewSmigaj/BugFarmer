## 3. `docs/guides/authoring/ant-colony.md` and `caves.md`

### `docs/guides/authoring/ant-colony.md` (underground ant zones)
Prescribes:
- **Nest shape** = "a **tree of narrow tunnels descending from a surface entrance into chambers** dug through soil." Fill solid rock mostly with `dirt_block` + minority `stone_block` — "**Soil, not bedrock**… **no ore veins.**"
- **Entrance** = an `ant_mound` at the top edge over the main shaft mouth.
- **Main shaft** = a single **narrow (1-cell), meandering** tunnel dropping to the **queen's chamber**.
- **Branches** = short narrow tunnels to **chambers** (2–4 cells): **queen's chamber** (largest/deepest), **brood chambers**, **food stores** — "A nest branches like a root system."
- **Trails = files of ants** — place `ant_worker` on a tunnel's **ordered centerline**, ~every 2 cells with jitter; main shaft busy, side branches a few; `ant_queen` in the queen's chamber, 2–3 workers per chamber.
- Uses only **blocks + ant sprites** (`ant_worker`, `ant_queen`, `ant_mound` already exist); `place_bug` (free-floating). Worked example: `tools/zonegen/scenes/scene_ant_colony.py`. Tuning tells: shaft `bias≈0.16` + `wobble≈0.7`, preview scale ≈2.4 (queen ≈3.2). Future promotion to `features/ant.py` (`dig_nest`, `ant_file`). **No per-zone ore table** (ant soil has no ore).

### `docs/guides/authoring/caves.md` (tunnels, caves, water, ore)
Prescribes the underground **primitives** in `tools/zonegen/features/cave.py`:
- **Tunnels**: `carve_tunnel(b, start, end, style="natural"|"straight")` — natural **meanders** (width 1–3, never straight >~4 cells); man-made **straight** must carry `mine_rail` + `mine_support`.
- **Caverns**: `carve_cavern(...)` (shapes: blob/long/gallery/rocky/lobed; fBm + cellular-automata smoothing) and `carve_chamber(...)` (2–3 offset blobs). Cave mouth = cavern clipping the zone edge. **"Isolation is the point"** in a mining zone (don't auto-connect caverns) — *"Ant burrows through soft dirt are the exception — that's the colony's habitat."*
- **Water**: `cave_pool(...)` (irregular, off-centre), underground streams meander.
- **Ore doctrine (§4)**: `fill_solid(b, carved, ore_table, substrate=)` — **ore as VEINS (3–6 cells), jittered-grid seeding, commons shallow / rares few + `deep`**; **MEASURE** (~13–16% ore-bearing, deep fifth 20–25%, mean dist-to-ore 3–4 cells). Owner corrections **C2/C3** inline: *"'dirt areas' means DIRT-BLOCK MASSES, not painted ground"* and *"Ore is ONLY veins… never hand-set, even rares."* One rock only (`stone_block`); `hard_stone_block` removed.
- **Per-zone ore table** (§4, `underground_passages_31`): table with roles — `dirt_block` = *"the surface band + **the SW ant seam**"*; `stone_block` base; copper/coal common all depths; iron `any` baseline; tin sparse mid; silver bottom+deep; **gold/platinum/diamond deep only**.
