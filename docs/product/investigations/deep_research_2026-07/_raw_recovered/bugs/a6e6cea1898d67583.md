## PER-SPECIES DISTILLED BULLETS

### HORNETS

**European hornet (*Vespa crabro*)**
- **Behavior**: carnivorous hunter in spring → scavenger in fall; unusually active at night / attracted to light.
- **Aggression trigger**: nest defense + food-source defense; stings when grabbed/stepped on.
- **Recruiting**: performs an "alarm dance" (buzzing, darting in/out) outside the nest; alarm pheromone 2-methyl-3-butene-2-ol rallies defenders.
- **Range/speed**: not quantified in source; large (workers 25mm, queen 35mm).
- **Avoid/repel**: don't grab/disturb; a light source pulls them at night; smoke masks the alarm pheromone.

**Asian giant hornet (*Vespa mandarinia*)**
- **Behavior**: apex predator; autumnal **"slaughter phase"** — decapitates honeybees; **1 hornet = up to 40 bees/min**, **<50 hornets wipe out a 10,000s-strong hive in hours**.
- **Trigger/recruiting**: a **scout** lays a pheromone trail to lead nestmates to a hive → group raid.
- **Speed/range**: **40 km/h (25 mph)** flight; **100 km/day**; forage radius 1–2 km (up to 8 km); **6mm stinger**, repeated stings, potent neurotoxic venom.
- **Counter (great mechanic)**: the Japanese honeybee **heat-ball** — ~100 bees cook it at **46 °C** + CO₂. Player counter = trap-and-overwhelm.

**Bald-faced hornet (*Dolichovespula maculata*)**
- **Behavior**: builds a large **aerial paper nest (up to 58 cm)**; colony **400–700**.
- **Trigger**: aggressive when the nest is approached or near dwellings.
- **Defense (unique)**: **repeated stinging** + can **spray venom into eyes → temporary blindness** (a ranged attack).
- **Avoid/repel**: keep distance from the aerial nest; clear at night.

### WASPS

**Yellowjacket (*Vespula* ground / *Dolichovespula* aerial)**
- **Behavior**: *Vespula* nests **underground** (old rodent burrows), *Dolichovespula* **aerial**; colonies 4,000–15,000 cells (some to 100,000).
- **Trigger**: **vibration** (mowers/clippers), **CO₂ spikes**, dark colors, and nest disturbance; **"most dangerous, unpredictable"** US stinging insect.
- **Recruiting**: **sting repeatedly**; **mark aggressors and pursue**; crushed body releases alarm pheromone that summons nestmates; **chase up to a few hundred feet (rarely ~a mile)**.
- **Late-season aggression**: fall food shortage → aggressive **scavenging at sugar, fruit, meat, garbage**.
- **Avoid/repel**: don't swat/crush (releases pheromone); stay light-colored & still; clear nests **at night**; smoke masks alarm signal.

**Paper wasp (*Polistes*)**
- **Behavior**: open **umbrella comb** nest under eaves; hunts **caterpillars** + nectar; **least aggressive** of the group.
- **Trigger**: only attacks if it or the nest is directly threatened.
- **Tell (great readable escalation)**: **3-stage** — raise wings (posture) → patrol nest surface → fly out and sting.
- **Avoid/repel**: simply don't disturb; alarm pheromone coordinates defense once triggered.

### MOSQUITOES

- **Host-seeking**: **CO₂ plume** (responds >500–600 ppm) is the primary long-range cue; adds 1-octen-3-ol, sweat odors (nonanal/sulcatone/lactic acid), **body heat**, and **dark/high-contrast visuals**.
- **Flight (the signature mechanic)**: **cast-and-surge** — surge straight upwind while in the scent plume, **cast side-to-side in 30–60° arcs (~2–3 s)** when it loses the trail → a **telegraphed wavy zig-zag approach**; slow flier (~0.5–2 km/h; *Anopheles* up to 12 km/night).
- **Feeding**: **only females** bite for a blood meal; weak flier that lingers near breeding water.
- **Timing/variants**: mostly **crepuscular** (dawn/dusk); **Aedes/tiger bite by day** and strongly prefer humans; disease vectors (malaria, dengue, Zika, yellow fever).
- **Avoid/repel**: break the scent line (the cast-surge visibly fails), wear light colors, avoid dusk/standing water, reduce heat/CO₂ signature.

### FLIES

**Housefly**
- Erratic **looping, agile flight**; **sees ~7× faster than humans** → dodges slow swats.
- Lays **75–150 eggs** on rot/carrion/feces; clouds around food and filth; mechanical vector of 100+ pathogens.
- Game read: **non-damaging nuisance/evasion enemy** — hard to hit with a slow swing, contaminates crops/food.

**Horsefly (Tabanidae)**
- **Only females bite**, slicing flesh (painful); locate hosts by **polarized light off dark, moving objects** (not heat-at-distance).
- **Very fast** (a male clocked at **145 km/h / 90 mph**); **daytime/sunlight only, inactive at night**; chase large moving mammals.
- Game read: **fast daytime melee chaser** keyed to movement + dark color; escape via shade / breaking sight, not outrunning.

**Botfly (Oestridae)**
- **Parasitic larvae** develop in host flesh (myiasis); *Dermatobia hominis* uses **phoresy** — catches a **mosquito/fly and glues 15–30 eggs to it**; **host body heat triggers hatching**, larva drops & burrows.
- Game read: **two-stage parasite** — hijacks a carrier bug as its delivery vector; kill the carrier or take a lingering parasite/infection debuff.

**Fruit / vinegar flies vs. spotted-wing drosophila (*D. suzukii*)**
- Common vinegar flies: **cloud around overripe/rotting/fermenting fruit only**.
- **Spotted-wing (the crop pest)**: **serrated ovipositor lays eggs in RIPE, undamaged fruit** — cherries, berries, grapes, peaches; **300 eggs/female**, **13 generations/season**, **$700M** US losses.
- Game read: the real **farming antagonist** — ruins the player's *ripening harvest*, creating a harvest-before-infestation race.

## STANDOUT GAME-MECHANIC TAKEAWAYS (the "readable" wins)

1. **Mosquito cast-and-surge = a telegraphed wavy approach.** The single most readable enemy motion in the set — the player literally watches it hunt the scent plume in zig-zags and can dodge by breaking the line.
2. **Alarm pheromone = a "recruit more attackers" system.** Disturb a nest → escalating waves. Crushing/swatting *adds* attackers (pheromone release). Yellowjackets even **mark** you for the whole colony.
3. **The smoker is the canonical counter** — it **masks the alarm pheromone** (breaking the recruit loop) and **pacifies via a gorging/evacuation instinct**. A perfect diegetic beekeeper item.
4. **Concrete, tunable aggro triggers**: dark/black target = high aggro (quantified: black draws ~26 attackers for 82s; light colors ~0); vibration/running = trigger; standing still + light + quiet = safe; **sound doesn't matter**; nests safest to clear **at night**; aggro has a **leash range** (few hundred feet) so escape is possible.
5. **Distinct enemy archetypes fall out naturally**: slow homing mosquito (heat/CO₂) vs. fast daytime horsefly (movement/dark) vs. evasion-gimmick housefly (can't be swatted) vs. crop-ruining spotted-wing vs. the giant-hornet **hive-raid boss** (slaughter phase) vs. the botfly **parasite-via-carrier**.

All facts above are cited only from the 14 sources I deep-read (Wikipedia species/topic pages, the University of Kentucky extension EF-620, and the peer-reviewed *Vespa velutina* defense study PMC8034368, plus the mosquito plume-tracking literature surfaced via search). Where a source did not quantify something (e.g., European hornet flight speed, mosquito engorge volume), I marked it as not specified rather than inventing a number.
