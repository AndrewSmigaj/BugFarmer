# How the best bug games pick and tier their creatures (bestiary research)

Research for Bug Farmer (2026-10-02). The owner has just reviewed the bug roster and wants a tasteful one: bugs players
recognize, or a few kinds of a recognizable bug (a family of one to three kinds), now and then a one-of-a-kind bug, every
bug part of an ecosystem, nothing players can't make sense of. This page studies how bug games and famous creature-roster
games choose and structure their creatures, and what makes players love or dislike a roster.

Research only: nothing here is decided.

Status: IN PROGRESS (written section by section as sources are read).

Method: four search angles (by game; by technique, e.g. how variants are designed; by player complaint and praise; by
developer interview). Every source in the source table was fetched and read in full, not taken from a search snippet.
Where a claim rests on one page, the page is named next to it.
