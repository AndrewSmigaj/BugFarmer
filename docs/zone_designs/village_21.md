# Zone Design: Starting Village (2,1)

## Overview

A large meadow zone with a small village settlement. Most of the zone is open meadowland with natural features. Players begin here and eventually purchase land plots from City Hall.

**Zone ID:** `village_21`
**Grid Position:** Row 2, Col 1
**Biome:** Village / Meadow
**Difficulty:** Easy

---

## The Village (small area, roughly center)

A compact settlement, not a sprawling town.

### Buildings

| Structure | Size | Purpose |
|-----------|------|---------|
| **City Hall** | Larger, distinct style | Buy land plots |
| **Merchant Shop** | Standard | General goods |
| **NPC Cottages** | 2-3 small | Village residents |

### Village Features

- Public workbench (basic crafting only)
- Signpost (fast travel)
- Well
- Flower rows, potted plants
- Stone path streets
- Fences around yards
- Torches/lamps

---

## Outside the Village

### Fly Farm (east or south of village)

- Fenced enclosure
- Plants inside
- Working fly farm example
- Farmer NPC

### Woodcutter's Cabin (north of village)

- Small cabin in tree cluster
- Woodcutter NPC (hire later)
- Logs, stumps around area
- Path to village

### Secret Merchant (remote location, TBD)

- Hidden cottage far from village
- Special rare items
- Player must explore to find

---

## Roads

3-cell wide stone paths to zone edges:

| Direction | To |
|-----------|-----|
| West | Bee Meadow (2,0) |
| North | Butterfly Fields (1,1) |
| East | Wasp Thicket (2,2) |
| South | Underground entrance (3,1) |

---

## Meadowland (majority of zone)

The village and features above are small. Most of the zone is open meadow:

- Grass ground with occasional dirt patches
- Scattered flowers (wild, red, yellow, blue)
- Tall grass patches
- Bushes
- Tree clusters (groups of 3-8, not dense forest)
- Occasional rocks
- Room for player exploration

---

## Spawn Point

On road near village center. Player immediately sees signpost and village.

---

## Not Present

- No player house (buy land later)
- No furnace, anvil, advanced crafting
- No dangerous bugs
